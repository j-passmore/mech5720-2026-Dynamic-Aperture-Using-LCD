# Depth estimation and reconstruction photos

Both depth methods and both reconstruction scripts read the photos in this folder.
[The depth README](../../App/Processing/I-Depth_Estimation/README.md) lists the files they expect.
`distances.yaml` gives the distance of each PSF. `Scale_1/mask1.CR2` and `Scale_1/mask2.CR2` hold
the scene pair the group shot on 25 October at 2 s, ISO 200 and f/5. The group has yet to copy in
the dark frames and the PSF photos.
